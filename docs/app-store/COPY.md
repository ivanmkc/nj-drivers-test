### Description (4000)

**Headline (first line shown in collapsed view):**
Practice for your driver's permit using questions grounded in real DMV manuals, not someone's best guess.

**Body:**
Practice with questions based on official state driver handbooks. Source references and available manual excerpts help you check explanations. This independent study tool does not reproduce or predict the official exam.

**What you get**

• **50 US jurisdictions.** All 50 US states are included; Washington, DC is not. Manual editions and verification dates are shown where available. Check the agency's latest handbook before your exam.

• **18,072 questions.** Across all states, every category your written test covers — signs and signals, right of way, alcohol and drugs, sharing the road, penalties, and the rest.

• **Source references.** Open the handbook from the iOS quiz or results screen. Supporting excerpts are shown where available, so you can check an explanation against its source.

• **Real road-sign images.** Every sign question shows the actual MUTCD sign (the same standard your DMV uses), not a cartoon approximation.

• **Offline, always.** Download once, study anywhere — no signal needed, no account, no ads, no tracking.

• **English and Spanish.** Choose your study language before starting a quiz. Exam-language availability varies by state; iOS warns when it is not confirmed in our source evidence.

• **Track your progress.** See where you're strong, where you're weak, and which categories deserve another pass before test day.

**Why "real manuals" matters**

The banks are generated from state driver manuals and checked by automated quality gates. Those checks and any displayed grades are not a guarantee that every driving rule is current or correct. Consult the licensing agency's current handbook and report a question when an explanation needs review.

**Privacy**

We collect nothing. No account. No analytics. No tracking. Your progress lives on your phone, in your phone's standard storage, and goes nowhere else. The app works fully offline after download.

**Got feedback?**

Report a problem at https://github.com/ivanmkc/nj-drivers-test/issues. Include the state, study language and question text or a screenshot.

## 7 · Support / help / FAQ copy

### Support email body template

> Thanks for getting in touch! Tell me which state you're studying for and what happened — if it's a wrong answer or out-of-date question, please include the question text or a screenshot, plus your study language so I can trace it back to the source paragraph and fix it.
>
> — Driver's Test Prep support

### One-paragraph FAQ — "Why is this free?"

> The app is open-source (https://github.com/ivanmkc/nj-drivers-test) and the questions are based on state government publications; those sources remain the property of their respective agencies. There's no server to pay for and no data to sell. If it ever needs funding it'll be transparent and optional — never paywalled, never ad-funded, never tracking.

### One-paragraph FAQ — "Why isn't DC included?"

> DC's DMV publishes its driver manual only as a JavaScript flipbook on Issuu, with no downloadable PDF. We can't extract the source text reliably enough to ground the question bank in it. We're tracking a fix that uses headless-browser scraping — see https://github.com/ivanmkc/nj-drivers-test/issues for status.
