### Description (4000)

**Headline (first line shown in collapsed view):**
Practice for your driver's permit using questions grounded in real DMV manuals, not someone's best guess.

**Body:**
Driver's Test Prep is the only permit-practice app where every single question traces back to a real paragraph in your state's official driver handbook. We don't write questions from memory or paraphrase old test guides — we read the manual your DMV actually publishes, and every question links back to the chapter and page it came from.

**What you get**

• **50 US jurisdictions.** Every state except DC, plus the latest published edition for each. From California's 2025 handbook to South Dakota's December 2023 revision, we use whatever your state currently publishes.

• **17,753 questions.** Across all states, every category your written test covers — signs and signals, right of way, alcohol and drugs, sharing the road, penalties, and the rest.

• **Source citation on every question.** Tap any question to see the exact handbook section it came from. You can verify our work, and you study with the same vocabulary your test will use.

• **Real road-sign images.** Every sign question shows the actual MUTCD sign (the same standard your DMV uses), not a cartoon approximation.

• **Offline, always.** Download once, study anywhere — no signal needed, no account, no ads, no tracking.

• **English and Spanish.** Switch languages mid-session; the question, choices, and explanation all translate.

• **Track your progress.** See where you're strong, where you're weak, and which categories deserve another pass before test day.

**Why "real manuals" matters**

Most permit apps reuse the same recycled question banks that pass between study-guide publishers. They're often years out of date, and they paraphrase the rules instead of teaching the exact language your DMV examiner uses. We started fresh: for every state we downloaded the current official driver manual, extracted the actual text, and built the question bank from that source. When you see a question about following distance behind a motorcycle, the citation links to the specific paragraph in your state's handbook — same words, same emphasis, same examples.

**Privacy**

We collect nothing. No account. No analytics. No tracking. Your progress lives on your phone, in your phone's standard storage, and goes nowhere else. The app works fully offline after download.

**Got feedback?**

We track every reported issue and update banks as states republish their manuals. Email support@<TODO_DOMAIN> or open an issue at github.com/<TODO_REPO>.

### One-paragraph FAQ — "Why is this free?"

> The app is open-source (github.com/<TODO>) and the questions are derived from public-domain government publications. There's no server to pay for and no data to sell. If it ever needs funding it'll be transparent and optional — never paywalled, never ad-funded, never tracking.

> DC's DMV publishes its driver manual only as a JavaScript flipbook on Issuu, with no downloadable PDF. We can't extract the source text reliably enough to ground the question bank in it. We're tracking a fix that uses headless-browser scraping — see github.com/<TODO>/issues for status.
